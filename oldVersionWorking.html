Best viewing spots

South Arm Peninsula, Clifton Beach, Cape Bruny — away from Hobart's city lights and with clear southern horizons.

📸

Photography tip

Even at Kp 5–6 a camera can capture aurora invisible to the naked eye. Use ISO 800–3200, f/2.8, 10–20 s exposures.

🌒

Moon & darkness

A new moon is ideal. Observe 1–2 hrs after sunset when the sky is fully dark and before the moon rises.

⚡

Solar cycle note

We are near Solar Cycle 25 maximum (2024–2026), meaning higher-than-average aurora activity — a great window for sightings.

Viewing season — best nights: March to September, with peak activity around equinoxes. –
